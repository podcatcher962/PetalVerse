## 中文

飞花令（PetalVerse）是一款免费、开源的古典诗词赏玩工具，收录 **24 个高频令字、318 句经典诗句**，带你重温古人行令雅趣。纯文学、零玄学。

### 特色

- **24 个令字**：花、月、风、雪、春、秋、酒、山、水、云、雨、夜、梦、心、人、天、江、柳、梅、竹、楼、舟、家、愁，一键切换
- **句句有料**：每句诗都配完整全诗、作者、诗名、英文翻译、白话解读
- **点击看解读**：点任意一句，弹出全诗与赏析
- **随机抽句、星标收藏**：喜欢的诗句一键收藏
- **中英双语、双主题**：羊皮卷亮色 / 暗色蓝，默认亮色
- **纯文学，零占卜**

### 玩法

飞花令是中国古代酒令之一，源出唐代韩翃《寒食》「春城无处不飞花」。行令者依次说出含指定「令字」的诗句，不可重复，接不上者饮一杯。

### 免责声明

本软件仅供中华古典诗词的学习、赏析与娱乐，不含任何占卜、预测、迷信内容，亦不用于商业用途。所载诗句、译文与解读均整理自公开文献，仅供参考，可能存在疏漏，欢迎指正。

### 技术说明

单文件 HTML，无后端、无依赖、无追踪，离线可用，浏览器直接打开即用。在线版：<https://podcatcher962.github.io/PetalVerse/>

## English

PetalVerse (Feihua Ling) is a free, open-source classical Chinese poetry companion featuring **24 keywords and 318 classic verses**. Pure literature, no divination.

### Features

- **24 keywords**: flower, moon, wind, snow, spring, autumn, wine, mountain, water, cloud, rain, night, dream, heart, people, sky, river, willow, plum, bamboo, tower, boat, home, sorrow
- **Rich content**: every verse comes with the full poem, author, title, English translation and a plain-language note
- **Tap for details**: tap any verse to reveal the full poem and commentary
- **Random draw & favorites**: save verses you love
- **Bilingual & dual theme**: Chinese/English, parchment-light / dark-blue, light by default
- **Pure literature, zero fortune-telling**

### How to play

Feihua Ling is an ancient Chinese drinking game, named after Han Hong's poem "Spring city, nowhere without flying flowers." Players take turns quoting verses containing the chosen keyword, without repetition.

### Disclaimer

This software is for the study, appreciation and enjoyment of classical Chinese poetry only. It contains no divination, prediction or superstition, and is not for commercial use. Verses, translations and notes are compiled from public sources for reference and may contain errors.

### Technical notes

Single-file HTML with no backend, no dependencies and no tracking. Works offline in any browser. Live version: <https://podcatcher962.github.io/PetalVerse/>
